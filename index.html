Frequently asked questions

What do I do after I pay?

Contact us on Discord. Right after you send payment, message us on Discord with your Cash App receipt and the package you ordered so we can verify it and get your account delivered. Delivery does not start until you have reached us, so send that message as soon as you have paid.

What happens if I send the wrong amount?

No account is delivered. Payment verification is manual, and an order is only fulfilled once the exact package price has been received. A short payment, an overpayment, or one order split between the two recipients will not be fulfilled. If you have paid the wrong amount, contact us on Discord and we will arrange a refund with you.

Which platform is supported?

PC account packages only. Console accounts and console services are not offered on this storefront.

Are accounts guaranteed against bans?

No. No seller can guarantee that a modified account will never be restricted or suspended. Account modification or resale may violate game or platform terms and can result in suspension or loss of access.

What is the refund policy?

Refunds are available. If you are not happy with your order, contact us on Discord and we will sort it out with you. Send your Cash App receipt and the package you bought, and we will take it from there. Refunds are handled manually, so allow some time for a reply.

How is payment verified?

Verification and fulfilment are manual. This page cannot independently confirm a completed payment, so keep your Cash App receipt until delivery lands.

Have the prices changed?

Yes — the current prices are the lower ones shown on each package card. The struck-through figure is the previous price. Always send the amount shown on the card for the package you chose.
